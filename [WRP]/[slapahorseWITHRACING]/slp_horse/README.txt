*** Advanced Horse Script ***


Requirements:
- ESX or QBCore Framework
- PolyZone
- ox_lib


INSTALLATION: 
- Ensure both of the scripts in server.cfg 
ensure slp_horse
ensure slp_horsestable
- Please choose the correct clothing for your server on the config.lua
- Install slp_horsestable/player_horse.sql in your database
- Configure your notification event in client/cl_shared.lua
