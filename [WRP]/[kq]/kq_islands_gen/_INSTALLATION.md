# KQ_ISLANDS_GEN Installation guide

## Step 1
Install `kq_islands_props`. You should be able to find this resource on your keymaster.

You've received it automatically when purchasing `kq_islands_gen`

(Make sure to ensure `kq_islands_props` before `kq_islands_gen`)

## Step 2

Ensure the script in `server.cfg`


## Step 3 (Optional)

Edit the config to your likings.

You can add more islands. Change their seeds, foilage density values and much more

*You'll probably want to go over the islands after changing their seed*

### Suggestions/Use cases
- Add drug locations to the islands
- Add special NPCs to the islands that will sell players unique items
- Use islands for gangs to grow drugs
- Throw parties or special scavenger events on the islands
